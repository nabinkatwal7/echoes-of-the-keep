PARTIAL NOTES
f85: light sources burn down with the clock and ambient light follows the time of day; falling, suffocation and starvation rules exist in the engine but have no table UI yet (the DM applies damage by hand)
f90: encounter builder uses a simple rule of thumb (the handbook has no encounter tables) and the monster list is only the 31 Appendix D creatures
f94: scenes are https image backdrops shown to the whole table, plus the combat grid; there is no token-based exploration map
f76: timed effects: conditions from spells carry round timers and drop with concentration; Bless, Shield and Shield of Faith are tracked as buffs; other buff spells are narrated
f77: summons join the party's side as allies; Polymorph swaps stats and can be reverted by the DM (not part of the undo snapshot, and the form does not revert on its own at 0 HP)
f78: utility, exploration and social spells spend the slot and are logged; their effects are narrated (see docs/spell-automation.md for the list)
f80: Divine Smite and Sneak Attack are automated; Metamagic, invocations, domain features and other class features are not
f82: 32 percent of the 362 spells resolve automatically (run pnpm spells:coverage); the rest are narrated
f64: Ready records the trigger and spends the reaction on use; the DM decides when a trigger fires
f65: underwater rules apply (melee/ranged penalties, fire resistance handled in rules); mounted combat is not built
f67: multiattack and flat/dice attacks are read from stat blocks; recharge and legendary actions are not built
f63: opportunity attackers are reported on movement; the attack itself is made by the player or DM with the Opportunity button
f4: Playwright e2e not set up yet
f15: only the 31 Appendix D creatures; SRD monsters still to import
f36: level-up is generic (HP average, ASI or feat, multiclass); subclass choice and new class features per level not applied yet
f44: campaign name and premise only; house-rule settings not built yet
f47: table page with chat and dice log; presence list and shared scene not built yet
f48: SSE stream polls the database every second with Last-Event-ID resume (verified over HTTP); LISTEN/NOTIFY not used yet
f50: chat, emotes and whispers to the DM; player-to-player whispers not built
f10: subclass level tables per subclass (domain spells, etc.) are not split out; subclass text is shown as written
f33: wizard covers race, subclass, class, abilities, background, skills, equipment (kit or gold) and details; racial effects like Hill Dwarf HP are not applied
f20: effects registry covers racial HP/speed/darkvision/resistances, feats Tough/Alert/Mobile/Observant, bard Jack of All Trades, barbarian Fast Movement, monk Unarmored Movement; other features are shown as noted traits
