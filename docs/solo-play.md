# Solo play

Play without a group. Open **Solo play** in the top menu and choose where you sit; the computer fills the rest.

| You are | The computer plays | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A player** | The Dungeon Master and 1-4 companion heroes | Play your hero in a short chapter: a hook, a first fight, a rest, a second fight, a rest, a final fight and an epilogue. Press the big button on the solo page to move the story on. |
| **The Dungeon Master** | 2-5 heroes in the party | Run the world as usual. Press *Easy / Medium / Hard fight* to have a suitable fight built for the party, or build your own. Computer heroes take their own turns. Tick *Let the computer play the monsters too* to sit back and watch. |

You can use one of your own heroes (levels 1-5) or a ready-made fighter, cleric, rogue, wizard, barbarian or paladin. The computer heroes are built with the same creation and level-up rules a person follows.

## How the computer plays

- **Heroes.** Heal someone who is down or under a third health (a bonus-action healing spell first, so the action stays free), bless the party on round one, cast an area spell when it catches two or more foes and no friends, otherwise pick the best spell or weapon attack against the foe it can hurt most, walking around walls to reach it. Casters save leveled slots for fights that matter and use cantrips otherwise.
- **Monsters.** Go for the foe they can kill or hurt most, take every attack of their Multiattack, and take opportunity attacks.
- **The Dungeon Master.** Chooses fights that fit the party level (easy, then medium, then a hard boss), narrates scenes in the style of the adventure (crypt, wilds, bandits or caves), shares small treasure, lets the party catch its breath with hit dice, and levels the computer heroes when a chapter ends. If the party is beaten it wakes at the edge of the place and the next try is gentler.
- Every action goes through the same rules and permission checks as a person's. Computer players have their own accounts (marked as bots, with no password) and show up in the table like anyone else, with a line of in-character chatter now and then.

## Limits

- Chapters are short and generated from templates. There is no branching plot, no social scenes and no dungeon map beyond the combat grid.
- Computer heroes use weapons, healing, damage spells and a few buffs. They do not use class features such as Rage, Sneak Attack, Divine Smite or Metamagic, and they do not use utility spells.
- Computer monsters do not cast spells or use special abilities.
- You can keep five solo games at once. Deleting a game, or your account, removes its computer players.
